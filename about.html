ABOUT PET CARE

More Than Just Pet Care.

We believe every pet deserves attention, care, and a happy life. Pet Care brings essential pet care services together in one convenient mobile application.

OUR STORY

Making Pet Care Easier, Every Day.

Taking care of a pet involves several responsibilities, from managing pet information and arranging appointments to finding nearby veterinary clinics and remembering important care tasks.

Pet Care was developed as a mobile application to bring these essential services together in one place. It aims to make everyday pet care more organized and accessible for pet owners in Bahrain.

One convenient place to support the everyday needs of pets and their owners.
